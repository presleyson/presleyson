## About me

I am an Information Technology professional, professor and researcher based in Contagem, Minas Gerais, Brazil.

I have worked in IT since 1995, with experience across the service, finance, transportation and commerce sectors. Today I am CRO at [Prolinx](https://www.prolinx.com.br/), a company focused on business continuity and cloud solutions for its clients.

I hold a Ph.D. in Information and Communication Technology and Knowledge Management from Universidade FUMEC (2024). My thesis proposed the **AGITAR Canvas**, a technological innovation management model designed for small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs) in the Information and Communication Technology (ICT) sector. The software that puts the model into practice is published in this account.

Alongside my executive work, I teach in higher education, supervise undergraduate theses, carry out post-doctoral research, give talks and write about leadership, management and digital business.

### Research

My research follows a single line that began in my master's degree and continues today:

1. **Master's (2018 – 2020):** study of innovation management models in ICT SMEs, centered on MOGIT.
2. **Ph.D. (2021 – 2024):** proposal and application of the AGITAR Canvas as an evolution of MOGIT, with particular attention to the idea generation stage. The model was built from a systematic literature review and applied through multiple case studies in three ICT SMEs in Belo Horizonte. Data analysis used text mining in Python: unigram and bigram frequency, word clouds, sentiment analysis with the FinBERT-PT-BR model, and TF-IDF.
3. **Post-doctorate (current):** research on innovation management models for ICT SMEs, supervised by Prof. Dr. Luiz Cláudio Gomes Maia.

## Featured projects

### [AGITAR Canvas](https://github.com/presleyson/AgitarCanvas)

Digital implementation of the Technological Innovation Management Model proposed in my doctoral thesis.

- **Purpose:** a nine-block canvas that helps ICT SMEs organize, plan and track innovation projects on a single screen, using post-its for strategic planning, problem, market, idea generation, selected ideas, financial resources, technical resources, key partners and results.
- **Technologies:** JavaScript (JSX), React 18, Create React App, Radix UI Dialog, CSS, Jest and React Testing Library, GitHub Actions, GitHub Pages.
- **Relation to my work:** this is the instrument through which the thesis model was taken to the companies that took part in the study, and it is described in Chapter 6 of the thesis. It remains connected to my current research on innovation management for ICT SMEs.
- **Live application:** <https://presleyson.github.io/AgitarCanvas/>

### [Infinitas](https://github.com/presleyson/finisetelagoas)

Website, sales CRM and operations management for Fini Sete Lagoas, the official Fini franchise in Sete Lagoas, MG, published at [infinitas.kids](https://infinitas.kids).

- **Purpose:** a single repository for everything that is live: the landing page and blog, a public quote form that feeds the CRM, a six-stage sales pipeline with PDF proposals, and an operations module covering inventory across two units, transfers, cash and safe control, losses, staff scheduling and team activities.
- **Technologies:** plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript (ES modules, no build step and no framework), Supabase (PostgreSQL, Row Level Security, Edge Functions and Storage), pdf-lib, Resend for e-mail, Google Routes API, GitHub Pages.
- **Relation to my work:** a production system for a real retail operation. It applies, in practice, subjects I work with and teach: software engineering, permission models and data protection, auditing and process management.
